Armando
de la Garza

"Armando de la Garza (Monterrey, 1973) is a Mexican visual artist recognized for his technical precision and conceptual approach to painting. For more than three decades, he has developed a distinctive visual language that combines classical techniques with critical reflections on identity, power, beauty, and contemporary culture. Through genres such as portraiture, landscape, still life, and object studies, his work explores the symbolic structures behind the images that shape our perception of reality. De la Garza has presented over 20 solo exhibitions and 40 group exhibitions internationally, participating in major art fairs including Zona Maco, ArteBA, Sydney Contemporary, and Art Madrid. His work has received multiple recognitions, including the Honor Mention and Acquisition Prize from the Studiolo Collection at Art Madrid 2025, as well as selections in prestigious Mexican art biennials such as the Bienal FEMSA and the Bienal Tamayo."

"Armando de la Garza’s work has been featured in institutional catalogs and reviewed by publications such as ArtNexus, La Tempestad, Vogue, GQ, and Travel + Leisure. He currently lives and works in Mexico City. His work is part of important public and private collections."
